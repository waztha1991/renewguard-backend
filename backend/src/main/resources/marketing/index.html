Capabilities

Built for how agents actually work

Capture once, track expiry, reach customers on the channels they answer, and stay productive without constant connectivity.

  • Native Android app

    Field-ready Android client with offline Room storage, Play/APK install, and cloud sync — the primary way agents work on the road.

  • Policy intake & documents

    Record customer, vehicle, and beneficiary details with NIC/VRC capture so renewals start from complete records.

  • Expiry dashboard & reminders

    See policies expiring in 30, 15, and 7 days — plus already expired — so follow-ups happen on time.

  • Call, SMS & WhatsApp

    Reach customers from policy detail with device-native call, SMS, and WhatsApp, and keep a reminder history.

  • Agent web portal & admin

    Same workflows in the browser at /app, with company control of agents and licenses at /admin.
